# Business Command Center

An all-in-one business operating system on a single screen.

## Features
- Business profile the AI uses in everything it writes
- Daily priorities checklist
- AI assistant chat
- Email studio: draft with AI, then open in your email app
- Workbook and course builder with a saved library
- Social media planner for drafting and scheduling posts

## How to use
Open `index.html` in a browser. Your tasks, posts and library are saved in your own browser.

## Notes
- The AI features work inside Claude. On a public site they need a Claude API connection.
- Posts are copied and pasted to social platforms. Direct posting isn't built in yet.
